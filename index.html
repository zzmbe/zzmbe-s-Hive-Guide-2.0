GENERAL ADVICE

Simple progression rules

1. Upgrade with a plan

Don't spend everything just because you can afford an item. Check what your next major milestone is first.

2. Keep your hive balanced

Prioritize useful bee abilities and event bees while building toward your longer-term hive direction.

3. Farm where your hive works best

Your best field isn't always the field with the highest base pollen. Match your bees, boosts, and abilities to the field.

4. Save for major milestones

Big gear upgrades can change your progression much more than lots of small purchases.
